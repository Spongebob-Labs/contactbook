## Scope

`/` — the Contact Book marketing one-pager. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience, job, action

Someone who hands their details to people often and has watched their network lose them after a
number, job or city change. They must believe two things in one scroll: their details will stay
reachable, and this company tells the truth about how. Action: create a card.

The second audience never reads this page — they scan a code. Every claim about the shared card
is made on their behalf.

## Proof on hand

Live product components (the wallet pass, the card page) that mount rather than being pictured.
No customers, no testimonials, no logos, no benchmarks. **Pricing is out of scope entirely.**
The broadcast is claimable in the present tense; the WhatsApp assistant is roadmap and must be
framed as what is next.

## Chosen direction — THE WALLET DECK

The page is a wallet. Each section is a pass that lifts off the stack as the reader reaches it.
Chosen by the user from a dealt hand of three at surface scope; the Micro world was already
pinned by the brief, so this round decided composition only.

## The memorable moment

⚠ **Rewritten. It was the hero film, and the client removed it along with the sky it stood in:**
*"the blue colour and the video being rendered there are both looking the worst of all… it
should be text, and behind that, you have to think about what you could add better than this."*

The hero is now the product's own object rather than a picture of one: **a sheet of ruled
contact-book paper**, with a margin rule down the left and the headline written on it as the
first entry. One ruled row is exactly one headline line box, so the type sits on the ruling
rather than in front of it, and the ruling begins at the margin rule the way it does on a real
ledger. The one authored moment is that margin rule drawing down on load — the page gets ruled,
then written on.

It costs no bytes, so the page's LCP candidate is text rather than a video poster, and it sets
up the argument two sections below (*"a paper contact book worked because somebody maintained
it"*) instead of contradicting it with weather.

**Why three heroes failed before it, recorded so a fourth gradient is not attempted:** each
rejection ("childish", "very big", "the worst of all") was read as a colour note and answered
with a new gradient. The ground was never the defect — a full-bleed coloured rectangle with
centred type is the shape every generated landing page ships, and centred type gives the eye no
axis to return to. The fix was alignment and structure, not hue.

## Why the previous build failed, recorded so it is not repeated

The world was pinned and the softest possible rendition of it was shipped: a flat two-stop sky
band with no meadow, dark type where the brief specified white, hairline separation everywhere,
motion rationed to two scroll moments, and nine product cards spread across the page. The brief
pinned the world, not its quietest reading.

## Unresolved

Real pricing. Whether the assistant ships before launch. Whether the wallet pass is Apple-only
at launch.
